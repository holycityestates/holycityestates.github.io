About the project

A new boutique building in Nachalat Achim with only a handful of private homes, one per floor, beside Sacher Park and a few minutes' walk from Rehavia and Shaarei Chesed.

Every floor is a single private home with four air directions, from garden apartments to a 325 m² duplex penthouse overlooking the park. Each home has private parking, a storage room, an elevator and sukkah balconies.

The location combines the calm of a quiet residential street with the heart of the city: King George and Bezalel streets, the Kahal Chassidim synagogue and all community services are close by.

Prices include VAT. Viewings and full plans by appointment.
