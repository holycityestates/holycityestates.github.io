About this property

The crown of the building: a 325 m² duplex penthouse on the top two floors, with four air directions and wide views over Sacher Park.

8 rooms over two levels, with 57 m² of terraces, three parking spaces and a storage room. The lower level holds the main living spaces and bedrooms; the upper level adds two more bedrooms and a private terrace.

Finished to a luxury specification: underfloor heating, full VRF air conditioning with smart control, smart-home lighting and blinds, a smart entrance door with Shabbat mode, and premium kitchen and bathroom finishes.

Prices include VAT. Viewings and full plans by appointment.
