About this property

A new three-bedroom apartment on the 4th floor of a boutique TAMA building on Shmuel HaNagid, open to the east, north and west.

106 m² with a master suite, two more bedrooms (one of them the safe room), two bathrooms and a guest toilet. The living and dining space opens onto a 15.5 m² covered balcony, with a separate 5 m² sukkah balcony.

The apartment comes with a storage room, and parking is available for purchase. Expected delivery around March 2027. The apartments are delivered as a shell; finishes are negotiable with the developer.

Viewings and full plans by appointment.
